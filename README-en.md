## AI assistants

The most used, in alphabetical order. The order is not a recommendation.

- [ChatGPT](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-chatgpt) - OpenAI's assistant, with free and paid plans. `free` `paid`
- [Claude](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-claude) - Anthropic's assistant, with free and paid plans. `free` `paid`
- [Copilot](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-copilot) - Microsoft's assistant, also inside Windows and Office. `free` `paid`
- [Gemini](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-gemini) - Google's assistant, also inside Gmail and Google Docs. `free` `paid`

## Privacy in AI

Where to turn off the use of your conversations to train the model.

- [Anthropic: onde mudar o treino](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-anthropic-onde-mudar-o-treino) - How to stop your conversations with Claude from being used to train the model. `official`
- [Google: atividade do Gemini](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-google-atividade-do-gemini) - How to turn off Gemini's activity history and what changes with it. `official`
- [Microsoft: controles de privacidade do Copilot](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-microsoft-controles-de-privacidade) - Where the privacy and training controls of Copilot are. `official`
- [OpenAI: controles de dados](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-openai-controles-de-dados) - How to stop conversations on ChatGPT from being used to train the model. `official`
- [WhatsApp: privacidade](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-whatsapp-privacidade) - What WhatsApp says about privacy, including when you talk to Meta AI. `official`

## Writing and documents

The standard that applies when the document is formal.

- [CBO, busca por código](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-cbo-busca-por-codigo) - The Brazilian Classification of Occupations, to check the description of a job title. `official` `free`
- [Manual de Redação da Presidência da República](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-manual-de-redacao-da-presidencia-d) - The official writing manual, a reference for official letters, memos and formal communication. `official` `free`

## Scams that reach the office

Fake invoices, account switches and supplier emails that are not from the supplier.

- [CERT.br: fascículo golpes](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-cert-br-fasciculo-golpes) - The CERT.br Cartilha booklet on internet scams. `official` `free`
- [FBI IC3: fraude de e-mail corporativo](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-fbi-ic3-fraude-de-e-mail-corporati) - How the supplier or boss email fraud works, as described by the FBI. `official` `free`
- [Febraban: alertas de golpes](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-febraban-alertas-de-golpes) - Febraban's alerts about scams against companies and people. `official` `free`
- [Ministério da Justiça: sofri um golpe](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-ministerio-da-justica-sofri-um-gol) - What to do after falling for a scam, according to the Ministry of Justice. `official` `free`

## Data protection

The law and the authority that apply to customer and student data.

- [LGPD](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-lgpd) - The General Data Protection Law, in the compiled text from Planalto. `official` `free`
- [ANPD](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaesc-anpd) - The National Data Protection Authority, with guides and regulations on the LGPD. `official` `free`

## Tags

Each item says what it is. The tags next to it help you choose: official when it comes from whoever sets the standard, open source when the code is published, free or paid based on the usage model, API and SDK based on how you integrate. Items tagged "by the author" are made by Ciro Cesar Maciel and follow the same rules as all the others.
